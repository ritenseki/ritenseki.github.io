About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at North China University of Technology (NCUT), based in Beijing.

My research interests lie at the intersection of foundation models, computer vision, and interdisciplinary applications, specifically along two tracks:

  • Computational Philology & Digital Humanities: Applying Low-Resource NLP, knowledge graphs, and computer vision to ancient languages (e.g., Semitic languages, Ancient Greek) and the historical/religious evolution of the Ancient Near East and Middle East.
  • Embodied AI & Autonomous Perception: Real-time visual perception, 3D scene understanding, and robotic decision-making in physical environments.

💼 Open to Opportunities: I am actively seeking Research Internships (RA) or engineering roles (in Beijing or Remote) related to Computer Vision, Embodied AI, NLP, or Digital Humanities. Feel free to reach out via tianshuo.li.cn@gmail.com.
